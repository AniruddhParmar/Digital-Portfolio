01 Origin · Kutch, Gujarat

Bridging technology, trade & trust.

I'm Aniruddh Parmar — a Computer Science Engineer from Gujarat Technological University with a passion for bridging technology, trade, and entrepreneurship. My journey spans hardware & networking, digital forensics, and building Arcreed Exim House from the ground up.

Arcreed Exim House is my vision of what international trade should be — built on integrity, trust, and an unwavering commitment to quality. We don't just move products; we build relationships that last across borders.

Beyond trade, I'm deeply invested in digital forensics and cybersecurity — believing that in modern business, trust is verified, not assumed. The same rigor I apply to evidence analysis shapes how I approach every export deal.
